CHAPTER 353 TAX ABATEMENT AGREEMENT
=====================================

Property: Sunset Apartments
Address: 456 Oak Avenue, Houston, TX 77002
Tax Parcel ID: 123-456-789-0001

AGREEMENT TERMS:

Section 1: Tax Exemption Schedule
----------------------------------
The City of Houston hereby grants the following property tax abatement:

Years 1-10:  100% exemption from ad valorem property taxes
Years 11-20: 50% exemption from ad valorem property taxes
Years 21-25: 25% exemption from ad valorem property taxes
Years 26+:   Standard county tax rate applies

Section 2: Base Tax Rate
-------------------------
Harris County Standard Rate: 2.3% of assessed value
City of Houston Rate: 0.6% of assessed value
Combined Standard Rate: 2.9% of assessed value

Section 3: Effective Tax Rates with Abatement
----------------------------------------------
Years 1-10:  0.0% (100% abatement)
Years 11-20: 1.45% (50% of 2.9%)
Years 21-25: 2.175% (75% of 2.9%)
Years 26+:   2.9% (full rate)

Section 4: Conditions
---------------------
4.1 Property must maintain minimum 90% occupancy
4.2 At least 20% of units designated as workforce housing
4.3 Annual compliance reporting required
4.4 Breach of conditions results in abatement termination

Section 5: Financial Impact
----------------------------
Estimated annual tax savings (based on $10M assessed value):
- Years 1-10: $290,000/year saved
- Years 11-20: $145,000/year saved
- Years 21-25: $72,500/year saved

Total 25-year savings: $4,475,000

Executed: January 15, 2023
Effective Date: January 1, 2023
Expiration: December 31, 2047

City of Houston Economic Development Department
